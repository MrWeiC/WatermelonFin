Connection recovery

29 October 2026 · iPhone, iPad and shared tvOS implementation

Saved servers remain available when a home network cannot be reached. Connection attempts show progress and provide a conditional Tailscale/VPN reminder, without treating a network outage as an expired login.

BeforeAfter
Saved-account taps provide no clear progress; unreachable servers send users toward discovery.Connecting indicator, duplicate-tap protection, saved account retained, and Retry guidance.
No persistent explanation when connectivity is lost while browsing.Reachability checks while active, an inline notice below navigation, and automatic recheck on return.
Home can remain in its earlier error state after reconnection.Reconnection refreshes failed Home content and clears the notice.

Verification

Automated XCTest flow on iPhone 17 Pro Max and iPad Pro 13-inch simulators: sign in, simulate an unreachable server, check navigation remains accessible, retry successfully, switch accounts, verify progress and saved-account retention during failure, reconnect, then recover after background/foreground. iPad also asserts the notice heading sits below its top tab bar.

The outage is simulated by a loopback Jellyfin fixture with bounded request timeouts. No real VPN was disconnected and no server content was deleted. These tests do not certify physical-device VPN transitions or interrupted video playback. tvOS validation is a simulator build, not a remote-control UI run. English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese catalogs pass validation.

The pre-fix regression failed because the expected in-app guidance did not appear. The images below show the final implementation. Empty library areas are intentional fixture data. This branch is based on main; removal of Live TV and season navigation are in separate PR #41.
